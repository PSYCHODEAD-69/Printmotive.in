Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

PrintMotive ("we", "us", "our") operates the website printmotive.in (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information (Google Sign-In): When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google to create your account.
  • Profile & order details: Information you provide yourself, such as phone number, delivery address, state, city, and pincode, used to process and deliver your orders.
  • Order information: Products ordered, quantities, pricing, and any custom design files or images you upload for printing.
  • Reviews & media: Text, ratings, images, or videos you choose to submit as part of a product review.
  • Technical data: Basic technical information such as IP address, used only for security purposes like rate-limiting to prevent abuse of our upload and order forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process, fulfil, and communicate with you about your orders
  • To display your submitted reviews on our storefront
  • To maintain a server-side cart tied to your account
  • To protect our Service from spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In solely to authenticate your identity. We only request basic profile information (name, email, profile photo) and do not access any other Google account data.

4. Data Storage

Your information is stored securely on our servers, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access. Uploaded images and videos (for products, banners, and reviews) are also stored securely and served through our own content domain.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required to fulfil your order (e.g. sharing delivery details for shipping purposes)
  • When required by law

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • View and edit your profile information at any time from "My Account"
  • Remove orders from your personal order history at any time from "My Orders"
  • Request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at printmotive.in@gmail.com

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
